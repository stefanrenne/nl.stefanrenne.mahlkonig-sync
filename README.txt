Bring your Mahlkönig E64 WS into Homey. Every grind shows up with its dose, grind setting and grind time, and with a paired Sync Scale every shot follows with its yield, shot time and brew ratio.

Use it to log your espresso, nudge you when the beans run low, or turn the machine off a while after the last shot. The app reads your data from the Mahlkönig Sync cloud with your own Sync account. It is a community app and is not made by Mahlkönig.

Questions, ideas or problems? Visit the GitHub page linked on this app.
